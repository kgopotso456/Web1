Frequently Asked Questions

Can you build to custom dimensions?

Yes. We plan each project around your space, measurements, material preferences, and intended use.

How do I receive an accurate quote?

Submit an enquiry with your project details, approximate dimensions, and preferred materials. We can then discuss the scope and estimate.

Do you install projects on site?

On-site installation is available for cabinetry and fit-out projects. Installation requirements are included in the project estimate.
